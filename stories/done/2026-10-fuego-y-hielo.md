An evening where Josh and I went to a fire and ice evening. We biked 40 minutes to be in the
jungle. On the way there, there were big hills and big slopes, so we had to walk the bikes down
the hills because they were so high. When we arrived, the guy explained how it works. He has two
sauna tents and two freezers that he has converted into cold plunges. In the tents, he steams them
with garden herbs, so when you go inside, the air tastes delicious. We spent two hours over there.
It was just me and the boys, because there were four other men.
