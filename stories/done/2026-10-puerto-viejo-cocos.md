In Costa Rica, Josh wanted to make homemade coconut oil, and you can actually do that by finding
coconuts. So we decided to go out and he tried climbing a few trees, but they were too slippery and
too tall. We started wondering how people did it and looked on the floor to see if the coconuts
that were on the floor were maybe good enough. We found some, but when we opened them, they were
not good anymore.

Then the girl went and told us that we can actually get sprouting coconuts out of the ground, and
they would have what we needed inside to make coconut oil. You need the skin. The coconuts that
have sprouted have actually already used some of their skin as a nutrient to sprout. That has
somehow turned into a sponge that, instead of being liquid, takes on most of the space inside of
the coconut. We didn't know that until we opened it, and the texture is kind of like if you froze
ba ba ba ba, crazy. We haven't made coconut oil yet because there was not enough in the coconuts we
found, but we will one day.
